Privacy Policy — Darts & Wheel

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Français · English

Darts & Wheel is designed to run entirely on your device.

  • No data collection. The app does not collect, transmit or store any personal data on a server. There is no account to create and no server.
  • Everything stays on your phone. Games, scores, players, statistics and custom wheels are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves the device and is deleted when you uninstall the app, except for system backups you control (iCloud, Google backup).
  • No tracking, no ads. The app contains no advertising SDK, no ads, no analytics and does not use any advertising identifier (IDFA/AAID).
  • In-app purchases (tips). If you choose to leave a tip, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). The app never has access to your payment information. Tips are optional and unlock nothing.
  • No third-party sharing. No data is sold, rented or shared, since no data is collected.
  • Children. The app collects no data, including from minors.
